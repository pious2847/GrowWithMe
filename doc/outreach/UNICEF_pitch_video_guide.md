# UNICEF pitch video — script + recording guide (record TODAY, it's ~40 min of work)

## ⚠️ Why you must record a NEW video (cannot reuse the animated one)

The form says: *"The video must show the people building the solution, speaking for themselves, and the solution in action... Videos generated with AI, such as AI avatars, synthetic voices, or AI-generated footage, will not be assessed."*

Our beautiful animated demo uses a synthetic (Kokoro) voice → **disqualified for this field**. But basic editing is fine — you on camera + screen recordings of the real app + captions = perfect.

## Setup (10 minutes)

1. **Camera**: any phone, landscape, propped at eye level. Face a window (daylight). Plain wall background.
2. **Mic**: earbuds with mic, or phone close to you. Record a 5-second test and listen.
3. **Screen recording**: on your Android test phone — Settings → search "screen record", or swipe the quick-settings tile. Record the app flows below.
4. Wear something plain; TOVO logo behind you if you have it printed — optional.

## Script (≈115 seconds — read naturally, don't robot it)

**[ON CAMERA — 0:00–0:20]**
> "Hello, my name is Abdul Hafis Mohammed. I'm the founder and CEO of TOVO LABs, a Ghanaian software studio. With my team, I built GrowWithMe — an offline-first care companion for mothers and children under five in Northern Ghana, where distance, poor connectivity and low literacy keep families from timely care."

**[ON CAMERA — 0:20–0:40]**
> "GrowWithMe works fully without data. It gives mothers IMCI-aligned triage, WHO growth monitoring, a care calendar synced to Ghana's immunization schedule — and Nana, a voice-first AI educator that keeps working offline. Let me show you the real app, on a real phone."

**[SCREEN RECORDING — 0:40–1:40]** *(record these, then trim; narrate over them — you can record narration separately reading the bracketed lines)*
1. Home screen with a child registered → care calendar *(say: "A mother registers, adds her child, and the full care schedule builds right on the phone.")*
2. Open **triage**, pick symptoms, reach a verdict *(say: "When something feels wrong, adaptive IMCI questions return a verdict — low, moderate, or urgent.")*
3. Trigger an **urgent alert** *(say: "On urgent, one tap sends her patient report by SMS to the nearest CHPS compound, volunteer responders and the hospital — tracked to closure.")*
4. Pull down **quick settings → Airplane mode ON** → run triage again *(say: "And with airplane mode on, everything still works. No network needed.")*
5. Ask **Nana** a question by voice or text *(say: "Nana answers health questions in plain language — and she never diagnoses; deterministic clinical rules own every risk verdict.")*

**[ON CAMERA — 1:40–2:00]**
> "Everything you just saw is running today, built by us, in Ghana. With UNICEF's support we'll run a supervised district pilot with the Ghana Health Service and turn this working product into measured impact for mothers and children. Thank you."

*(That final segment is ~15s — total lands under 2 minutes.)*

## Edit + upload (10 minutes)

1. Phone edit: **CapCut** (free) — cut the clips in order, add auto-captions (allowed), light music off or very low.
2. Export 1080p.
3. YouTube app → Upload → visibility **Unlisted** → copy link.
4. Paste link in the form.

## Recording tips

- Do the on-camera parts in 3–4 short takes each; pick the best. Short takes = fewer mistakes.
- Look at the LENS, not the screen. Smile once at the start.
- Screen recordings: wipe the screen, full brightness, turn notifications off (Do Not Disturb).
- If you flub a line, pause 2 seconds and repeat the sentence — easy to cut.
- Name on YouTube: "GrowWithMe — TOVO LABs pitch (UNICEF Femtech Ventures)".
