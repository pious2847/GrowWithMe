## Project name
GrowWithMe — care that works where the network doesn't

## Amount requested
$15,000

## What is the idea / project? (short)

GrowWithMe is an offline-first mobile platform that keeps mothers and children under five alive and cared for in Northern Ghana — with the network completely off. It puts IMCI-aligned triage, WHO growth monitoring, immunization-schedule reminders with spoken voice, and a voice-first AI educator ("Nana") on a basic Android phone. When a child's condition is urgent, one tap SMSes a PDF patient report to the nearest clinic, volunteer responders and the hospital — and the referral is tracked to confirmed arrival. No data plan, no literacy barrier, no silent deaths between village and hospital.

## What makes it bold or unconventional?

Conventional digital health adds connectivity requirements and AI risk-scoring. We do the reverse: the entire core journey runs in airplane mode — including the AI's language understanding, in about one megabyte on-device — and a deterministic clinical rules engine, never the AI, owns every risk verdict. We bet that the technology most likely to save a mother is not the newest model, but the one that works on a $40 phone with no signal, speaks its reminders aloud, and closes the referral loop all the way to a human at a facility.

## What stage is it at?

Working prototype, fully built and internally verified end-to-end on real Android devices (offline triage, urgent-alert SMS dispatch with PDF reports, EPI-synced calendar, malnutrition flags, offline Nana). Code is public: github.com/pious2847/GrowWithMe. No field trial yet — Catalyst funds would seed the first supervised community pilot in Northern Ghana with community health workers.

## What will the funds enable (concrete)?

$15,000 covers the first supervised community pilot: SIM cards and SMS gateway costs for referral alerts and spoken reminders for ~300 families over 6 months; 10 basic Android phones for community health volunteers; printing of low-literacy quick-reference cards; and a small stipend pool for volunteer responders' data costs. Output: the first field evidence — alert-to-arrival times, reminder-to-visit conversion — in a shareable pilot memo.
