# GrowWithMe — Funders & Partners Outreach List (sender: TOVO LABs, team outreach)

**Sender identity:** all grant/partnership outreach goes out as **TOVO LABs** (Ghanaian software engineering & product studio) presenting **GrowWithMe** as its health product — "GrowWithMe, by TOVO LABs". Individual emails are sent personally by Abdul Hafis Mohammed, CEO, in the team's name. See `EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md` for the full sender block.

**Project one-liner (use everywhere):** GrowWithMe is an offline-first AI care companion for mothers and children under five in Northern Ghana — IMCI-aligned triage that works with no internet, WHO-growth & EPI immunization reminders, a voice-first local AI educator ("Nana"), SMS referral alerts that reach the nearest CHPS compound, volunteers and hospital, and a facility dashboard for health staff. Built with consent-first onboarding and full offline/low-literacy support.

**How to use this list**
- ✉️ = direct email found and verified from a public source → we can email via SMTP.
- 📋 = application/portal/form required → send through their page, not SMTP.
- ✋ = no public email found → **YOU SEND THIS ONE YOURSELF** (LinkedIn, phone, in-person, or a site contact form I could not verify).
- Ask = what we request: grant/funding, pilot partnership, technical/API partnership, or endorsement.
- Deadlines are as of 23 Oct 2026. **Confirm on their page before writing** — cycles change.

**Demo package available to attach/point to:** detailed 2¾-min funder demo video, 19 scenes incl. growth monitoring, facility dashboard and TOVO LABs team/ask (`demo-video/growwithme-outreach/renders/video-detailed.mp4` 1080p / `GrowWithMe-Demo-TOVO-LABs.mp4` 720p 8.7MB email attach), demo APK + install guide, feature roadmap (ROADMAP.md), "Nana" AI overview.
