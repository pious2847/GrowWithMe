The problem

Distance, connectivity and literacy keep families from timely care

Northern Ghana carries some of the country’s worst maternal and child health outcomes. Referrals die quietly between the CHPS compound and the hospital. Most digital-health pilots fail here because they assume a smartphone, data credit, or literacy the family doesn’t have. GrowWithMe assumes the opposite.

What’s inside

Built for the last mile, not the demo stage

Offline IMCI-aligned triage

Adaptive questions with deterministic danger rules for children and pregnancy — works with zero network. The rules engine, never the AI, owns every risk verdict.

WHO growth monitoring

Every weight plotted on WHO curves; slipping below −2 SD raises a malnutrition flag and schedules a catch-up visit.

EPI-synced care calendar

Immunizations, ANC/PNC, Vitamin A and deworming reminders synced to Ghana's schedule — spoken aloud for low-literacy mothers.

“Nana”, voice-first AI educator

A Ghanaian-elder persona for health questions and daily guidance, with a compact on-device model that keeps core commands working offline.

Closed referral loop

One tap generates a PDF patient report and SMSes it to the nearest CHPS compound, volunteer responders and the hospital — every alert tracked to closure.

Facility dashboard

Surfaces missed visits, growth faltering and lost-to-follow-up families, each with an SMS nudge — so nothing quietly disappears.

Engineering

Offline-first, voice-first, consent-first

Bounded AI: the LLM educates; a deterministic IMCI-aligned rules engine classifies every risk. Auditable by design.

  • Flutter offline-first architecture — local sync with last-write-wins and tombstones; the full core journey runs in airplane mode.
  • Hybrid AI — Llama 3.1 via NVIDIA NIM online; a compact fine-tuned on-device model keeps Nana’s core commands working offline.
  • Deterministic safety floor — clinical rules can raise a verdict, never lower one; every urgent path ends with a human.
  • Node/Express + MongoDB backend, Arkesel SMS gateway, Cloudinary for referral reports — keys proxied server-side, nothing sensitive on the device.
